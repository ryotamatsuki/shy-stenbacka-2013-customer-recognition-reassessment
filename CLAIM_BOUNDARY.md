# Claim Boundary

## Licensed claims

At the Stage-8 frozen scope, the project may state:

1. the exact pure-equilibrium validity threshold and complete pure \`UU\` correspondence for the directly inspected baseline model;
2. exact pure nonexistence for \`z_c<z<1/3\` in that baseline model;
3. existence and global-best-response validity of the displayed symmetric two-point mixed continuation;
4. corrected Stage-II investment thresholds and Stage-I sharing correspondence under the certified continuation;
5. corrected profit and consumer-surplus rankings and the surviving total-welfare ordering;
6. the source-specific Results 1–10 impact map;
7. that proof-critical algebraic identities have passed targeted Lean verification.

## Mandatory qualifiers

- high-\`z\` downstream claims: **under the certified symmetric two-point continuation**;
- portability: **MODEL-SPECIFIC**;
- formal verification: **PROOF-CRITICAL CORE**, not whole-model formalization;
- source equations: **directly inspected working-paper/predecessor model**, unless direct VOR comparison is later completed.

## Prohibited claims

- “the unique mixed equilibrium”;
- “the complete mixed-equilibrium correspondence”;
- selection-free high-\`z\` SPE/welfare results;
- generic pure-nonexistence theorem for customer recognition, switching costs, or common prices;
- portability to alternative switching-cost distributions or unequal type masses;
- “the published 2013 Eq. (8) is wrong” before direct VOR equation inspection;
- first-best or unrestricted social-optimum terminology;
- causal policy effects;
- whole-model formal verification.

## Change control

A substantive change outside this boundary reopens the earliest affected research stage and invalidates dependent Stage-8+ artifacts until recertified.
