## 2. Primitive timing and strategy map
The game has three stages.

1. Stage I: each firm chooses `S` or `NS`.
2. Stage II: each chooses `I` or `NI`, paying `c` iff it invests.
3. Stage III: firms choose incumbent and poaching prices permitted by the customer-information state generated by Stages I–II.

Consumers have valuation differences
[
d_{HH}=d_{LL}=0,quad d_{HL}=Delta,quad d_{LH}=-Delta,

[
