\section{Source and version scope}\label{sec:source}

The mathematical source used in this reassessment is the complete Federal Reserve Bank of Boston Working Paper 12-4, dated 15 February 2012, by Oz Shy and Rune Stenbacka \citep{ShyStenbacka2012}. The Boston Fed record identifies a revised version as having been published in \emph{Information Economics and Policy}, volume 25, issue 2, pages 92--106, in 2013. The journal article's bibliographic identity and DOI, \texttt{10.1016/j.infoecopol.2013.03.002}, are independently verified \citep{ShyStenbacka2013}.

This distinction matters because our available source evidence does not include a directly inspected equation-level copy of the version of record. The working paper supplies the complete timing, demand equations, price profiles, result statements, and welfare tables needed for a clean-room reconstruction. We therefore use its equation numbering only as a source map, not as evidence that the journal version contains text that we have not seen.

Throughout the paper, phrases such as "source profile," "source Result 6," or "the inspected model" refer to this verified working-paper version. We avoid the stronger statement that a particular equation in the 2013 version of record is erroneous. If a later direct comparison establishes equation-level identity, the attribution can be strengthened without changing the mathematics developed here; if the journal version differs materially, the source-impact map must be reopened.

This source qualification is orthogonal to the equilibrium argument. All propositions below are derived from the stated primitives rather than from a transcription of the historical audit that motivated the project.
