\section*{Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the manuscript preparation process}

During the preparation of this work, the author used OpenAI ChatGPT (GPT-5.6 Sol, accessed September 2026) to assist with manuscript organization, drafting, and language editing.  After using this tool, the author reviewed and edited the content as needed and takes full responsibility for the content of the publication.  AI-assisted literature/source triage, algebraic and code cross-checking, and proof-scaffolding support used as part of the research process are described separately in the research-verification methodology subsection.
