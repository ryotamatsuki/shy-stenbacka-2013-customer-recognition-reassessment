\section*{Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the manuscript preparation process}

During the preparation of this work, the author used OpenAI ChatGPT (GPT-5.6 Sol, accessed September 2026) to assist with literature discovery and source triage, algebraic cross-checking, development and review of Python/SymPy verification code, preparation and review of Lean proof scaffolding, manuscript organization, drafting, and language editing.  After using this tool, the author independently checked the underlying sources, mathematical derivations, executable verification scripts, formal proof outputs, and final manuscript text, edited the material as needed, and takes full responsibility for the content of the publication.
